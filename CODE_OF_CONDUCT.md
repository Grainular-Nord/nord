# Code of Conduct

Nørd welcomes contributors of every background, identity, and experience level. Everyone participating in the project, including maintainers, is expected to help keep the community respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code respectfully; keep criticism constructive and focused on the work.
- Respect different perspectives, personal boundaries, and privacy.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and respond thoughtfully to feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces, as well as public activities where someone officially represents Nørd.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to [sebastian@iamsebastian.dev](mailto:sebastian@iamsebastian.dev). Include what happened and any relevant links or screenshots. Please avoid posting sensitive reports in public issues.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled discreetly. Information will only be shared as needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content and take action proportionate to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Responses can include a request to correct behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may warrant an immediate ban.
